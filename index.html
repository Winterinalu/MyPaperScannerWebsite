How this was made

MyPaperScanner was created by one vibe coder with AI doing most of the implementation work needed to ship it. The human contribution was the idea, product direction, creative decisions, review, and refinement. The project source remains open for inspection, and the app stores documents locally without uploading them to an AI service.
